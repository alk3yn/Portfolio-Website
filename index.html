About Me

Hi, my name is Tejas Agrawal, an aspiring IT Software Engineer and Game Developer currently based in Takaoka, Japan. My journey into tech is a bit unique—I hold an MBA in IT and a B.Com, but my passion for programming led me to graduate from Tokyo Cool Japan with a specialization in game programming.

I have hands-on experience building games in Unity and Unreal Engine, and I developed this portfolio website entirely from scratch using plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I am highly adaptable and open to various IT domains, bringing foundational knowledge in databases like MySQL and strong logical problem-solving skills from my background in corporate auditing. Currently working at Alten Japan as a Semiconductor Equipment Engineer, I am actively building my skills to transition full-time into the IT or gaming industry. When I'm away from the keyboard, you'll find me exploring the Toyama area or watching anime (I've logged over 300 series!).
